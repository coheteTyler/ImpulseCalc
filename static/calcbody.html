1 · Gas conditions & velocity triangles
What this step is for
Pick the inlet relative FLOW the stage is designed for (velocity triangles), the machine identity (r_m, rpm, span → U, ṁ, power), and metal–flow mismatch (incidence / deviation).

Angle convention: β₁/β₂ below are design flow angles. Metal angles for §2 are β₁* = β₁ − i and β₂* = β₂ + δ. Triangles use flow; geometry uses metal.

Feeds §2–6: flow + metal angles, U, chord, solidity, |W₁|, p₁, T₁, r_m/rpm/span, ṁ/power, design name.
  1. Enter gas, flow angles, |W₁|, and either free U or U from rpm·r_m.
  2. Set span (and optional ṁ or power target) for stage capacity.
  3. Press Update triangles — check Mach, Euler work, tip Mach, ṁ/power.

4 · Run the cascade CFD test (mesh → solve → sample)
What “testing the turbine” means here
Runs CFD on the case folder built in §3 (geometry from §2, BCs from §1). You are not spinning a shaft in 3D — this is a 2D blade-to-blade cascade test: given this metal and inlet relative flow, how does the gas load the blade?
  1. Mesh — turn the §3 geometry into a grid (blockMesh).
  2. Solve — OpenFOAM-12 foamRun -solver shockFluid to the endTime from §3.
  3. Sample — surface pressure for §5 (PS/SS) and shock/loss analysis in §6.
